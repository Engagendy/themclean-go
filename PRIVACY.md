# Privacy Policy for theMClean Go

Effective date: May 9, 2026

theMClean Go is designed to review files and folders that you explicitly choose from the iOS or iPadOS Files app. The app does not require an account and does not upload your files to a server.

## Data Collection

theMClean Go does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

The app does not collect:

- Contact information
- Location data
- Identifiers
- Usage analytics
- Diagnostics
- Financial information
- User content
- Search history
- Browsing history
- Purchase history
- Advertising data

## File Access

theMClean Go can only access files and folders that you select through the system Files picker.

Selected files are reviewed locally on your device to calculate file size, category, modified date, and preview availability. Files are not uploaded, copied to a server, or shared with the developer.

iOS and iPadOS sandboxing prevents the app from scanning hidden system storage, private app containers, other apps, browser caches, Photos library content, Messages attachments, Mail storage, or operating system caches.

## Network Use

theMClean Go does not use external servers, cloud APIs, analytics services, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, payment processors, or AI services for its core functionality.

## Stage

The Stage feature is a review area inside the app. It helps you organize files before deciding what to do next. Staging files does not upload them to any server.

## Third-Party Services

theMClean Go does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, authentication, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

theMClean Go does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the app changes. Updates will be published in this repository.
